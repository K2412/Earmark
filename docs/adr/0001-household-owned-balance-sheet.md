# Keep the balance sheet household-owned and separate from transaction accounts

Earmark is built for two adults with combined finances, so financial positions and the household plan belong to a household rather than an individual user. Manual balance-sheet positions remain separate from future budgeting transaction accounts because property, mortgages, private-company equity, and other periodically valued positions do not share a transaction ledger's lifecycle; a later account integration may contribute calculated valuations through the same snapshot boundary.
