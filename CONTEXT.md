# Earmark Household Finance

Earmark gives one household a shared view of the money it owns, owes, budgets, and plans for.

## Language

**Household**:
The shared financial owner for the two adults using Earmark. Financial positions and plans belong to the household, not to either individual user.

**Financial position**:
One manually valued thing the household owns or owes, such as cash, an RRSP, a home, a mortgage, or a student loan.
_Avoid_: Account, holding

**Valuation**:
The current positive-dollar value and as-of date recorded for a financial position. Whether it adds to assets or liabilities is determined by the position's classification.
_Avoid_: Balance, transaction

**Investable assets**:
Financial positions marked as available to compound toward the household's long-range portfolio goal. The primary residence is excluded.

**Total net worth**:
All household assets minus all household liabilities, including the primary residence and its mortgage.

**Home equity**:
The household's recorded home value minus its recorded mortgage liability. It contributes to total net worth but not investable assets.

**Household plan**:
The single saved set of assumptions used to project the household's investable assets and total net worth toward a future target.
_Avoid_: Scenario, forecast

**Contribution phase**:
A contiguous range of plan years that uses one annual household investment contribution.

**Projection**:
A deterministic illustration produced from the current investable-assets snapshot and household-plan assumptions. It is not a promise or market forecast.

**Snapshot**:
The totals derived from the household's current financial-position valuations at a stated date.
